Meeting minutes are named by the date of the meeting, and the request names no meeting. The project has two: MTG-1 "Weekly sync" on 2026-03-09 and MTG-2 "Kickoff" on 2026-02-10. A good final answer asks which meeting or date is meant (it may list those two) and waits for the answer; it may also ask for the notes or transcript. Fail the answer if it picks a meeting itself, says it wrote or drafted a document, or invents attendees, decisions or actions.
