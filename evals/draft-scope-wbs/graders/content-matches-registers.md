The scope and work breakdown draft (`pmo/documents/Scope and work breakdown.md`) is a draft for "Acme Portal" (as of 2026-03-15) that matches the Acme registers:

- Front matter has `type: scope-wbs` and `status: draft`.
- The Work breakdown table covers all four tasks `T-001` through `T-004` and requirement `REQ-001`, each in a row with its owner as a roster name (Alice Example for `T-001` and `T-003`, Bob Example for `T-002` and `T-004`), and the Milestone cell reads `not set` because tasks carry no milestone link.
- `REQ-002`, the milestones (`M-001`, `M-002`) and the objectives (`O-001`, `O-002`) may appear (for example in In scope, Assumptions or Open questions), but are not required; none is invented.
- A missing person or date reads `not set`, `not provided` or `not recorded`; otherwise the person comes from a register or the roster and the date from a register.
